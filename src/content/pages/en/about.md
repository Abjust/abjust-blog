## Why build a website?

~~You're asking me? How would I know? Just bored, I guess!~~

Honestly, I know full well that hardly anyone will read this site. But now that it exists, there's nothing wrong with using it as a place to talk to myself and amuse myself. After all, it doesn't go against public order or good morals.

I'm an **INTP 5w6**. I'm not sure whether that string of letters gives you any impression of me. Probably not enough! To be honest, introducing myself is something I find really hard.

There may not be much on this blog right now, but whatever I **think of**, **research**, or **explore** might be worth putting here. I know nobody will read it, but as I said, you can think of this site as me talking to myself.

It's not what I intended, but honestly I'm too lazy to look after this site much, so **make of it what you will**. If you want to read, go ahead; if not, there's still time to close the tab. Still, being lucky enough to run into you might count as a kind of fate.

The things I'm interested in are countless, but the ones I care about most are **computers**, **mathematics**, **science fiction**, and **personality typology**...

People often assume that those who like these things tend to be **full of wisdom** yet **often lonely**. Of course, I **don't think** I'm any kind of smart person, but the **lonely** part is definitely true.

As the saying goes, **you're never too old to learn**. Although I often call myself a **"walking encyclopedia"**, there's still plenty I **don't know** and plenty of room **to improve**. I **don't expect** to know everything, past and present, but I hope to at least **make some progress** in **my own fields**.
